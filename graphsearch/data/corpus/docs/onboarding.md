# Engineering Onboarding

Welcome to the team. This guide points new engineers at the systems they will
touch first. For billing concepts, read the [[billing-overview]]. For how we sign
in to internal tools, read the [[auth-redesign]] spec.

## Who to ask

Billing questions go to the billing owner. Auth questions go to the auth owner.
